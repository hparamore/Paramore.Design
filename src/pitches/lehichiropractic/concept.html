Been in a car accident? You're covered in Utah.

Whether or not the accident was your fault, Utah law covers treatment for your injuries. We'll take care of you and the paperwork.

  • Dr. Miles documents your accident and injuries and builds a plan to get you back to normal.
  • We file everything your auto insurance requires.
  • If you need legal help, we'll point you to the right people.
  • Still no appointment needed. Come in during office hours to get started.
