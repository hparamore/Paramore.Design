What I’m great at.

Four things I’ve done at depth, not in theory — each one carried from first principle to shipped product, mostly as the only designer in the room.

01

Design systems

Tokens, components, and patterns built as one source of truth — so the product stays consistent at scale and changes propagate instead of getting re‑tweaked pixel by pixel.

I architect systems for adoption: clear naming, documented APIs, and the Figma‑to‑code link kept tight so design and engineering build from the same foundation.

02

Craft

The details that make a product feel inevitable — spacing, type, motion, and the interaction states most people skip. I sweat the line between “done” and “right.”

Accessibility and polish aren’t a final pass; they’re built into the component from the start.

03

Mentoring & leadership

I raise the bar for the people around me — through critique, pairing, and giving others room to own real work. The goal is multiplying output, not hoarding it.
