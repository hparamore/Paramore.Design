Design Services
Agreement

Effective November 12, 2026

This agreement is between Jordan Lee Design (“Designer”) and Maple Street Dental (“Client”). It covers the work described in Bid No. 2026-014, which is attached and is part of this agreement.

1. Services

Designer will deliver the work listed in the attached bid. Anything not listed there is outside this agreement. If Client asks for more, Designer will send a written quote, and that work starts only after Client approves it.

2. Timeline and client responsibilities

The work will take about four weeks from the deposit. Client will provide content, photos, logins and feedback within three business days of each request. Delays on Client’s side move the timeline by the same amount.

3. Fees and payment

The project fee is $3,300. A non-refundable deposit of $1,650 is due before work begins. The remaining $1,650 is due at launch, payable within 14 days of the invoice. Invoices more than 30 days late incur a late fee of 1.5% per month. Designer may pause work while an invoice is overdue.

4. Revisions

The fee includes two rounds of revisions at each stage (design and build). Additional rounds are billed at $85 an hour, with an estimate given before the work starts.

5. Ownership

When Client has paid in full, Designer transfers ownership of the final website design and files to Client. Until then, Designer owns all work. Designer keeps ownership of early concepts that weren’t chosen, and of the general tools and code used to build the site. Fonts, stock photos and plugins are licensed from their makers under their own terms. Designer may show the finished work in a portfolio.

6. Cancellation

Either party may end this agreement with written notice. If Client cancels, Client pays for work completed up to that date at $85 an hour, and the deposit is non-refundable. Designer will deliver whatever has been paid for.

7. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other’s private business information confidential and use it only for this project.

8. Limits

Designer is an independent contractor, not an employee. Designer is not responsible for problems caused by third-party services such as hosting, booking software or plugins. Designer’s total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees Client has paid.

9. General

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Utah. It is the whole agreement between the parties, and any change must be in writing and signed by both. Signatures sent electronically count as originals.
