Pricing worksheet

Price the project, not the hour. Answer these six before you send a number.

Client Project
01

Scope

What exactly is included, and what isn’t?

02

Their alternative

What would they do if you didn’t exist? Wix, the nephew, a big agency, doing nothing?

03

The value to them

What is the problem costing them now? Write it in their words, not design words.

04

Your floor

The number below which you say no. Decide it before the conversation starts.

05

The bundle

A starting price, a monthly line that keeps you in the relationship, and one or two add-ons tied to outcomes they care about.

06

Time check

Hours × the rate you’d accept. A sanity check, not the price you quote.
