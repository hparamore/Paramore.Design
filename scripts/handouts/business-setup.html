Business setup checklist

What my tax preparer told me to set up from day one. Check each one off.

File direct

Form your LLC with the Utah Division of Corporations (corporations.utah.gov): $59 to file, $18 a year to renew. Get your EIN free at IRS.gov.

Watch out: lookalike sites and official-looking emails charge $200–500 for the same forms, including yearly renewals.

Business account first

Every client payment lands in the business account, then you pay yourself. Never pay personal bills from it.

Fine: paying a business expense on a personal card. Just log it.

Keep every receipt

A phone photo in a folder counts. Card statements are a weak backup in an audit.

Track your miles

Every business drive is a deduction: $0.72 a mile right now. Log the date, where, and why.

Set money aside

25–30% of every payment if it’s just you. Closer to 20% with a spouse, kids, and real expenses. Put it somewhere you won’t touch.

Questions to ask a tax pro

  • Should my spouse be on the LLC? A single-member LLC pays self-employment tax on all of its profit. A non-working spouse partner can cut that in half.
  • When does an S corp make sense? Often around $40K a year in freelance net income.
  • Do I owe quarterly taxes? Once you’re past your first year.
  • What can I deduct? Lowering what you’re taxed on is one of the most important things you can do.
