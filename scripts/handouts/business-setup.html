Business setup checklist

What my tax preparer told me to set up from day one. Check each one off.

File direct

Form your LLC with the Utah Division of Corporations (corporations.utah.gov). Get your EIN free at IRS.gov.

Watch out: lookalike sites and official-looking emails charge $250–500 for the same forms, including yearly renewals.

Business account first

Every client payment lands in the business account, then you pay yourself. Never pay personal bills from it.

Fine: paying a business expense on a personal card. Just log it.

Keep every receipt

A phone photo in a folder counts. Card statements are a weak backup in an audit.

Track your miles

Every business drive is a deduction at the IRS standard mileage rate. Log the date, where, and why.

Set money aside

25–30% of every payment if it’s just you. Closer to 20% with a spouse, kids, and real expenses. Put it somewhere you won’t touch.

Questions to ask a tax pro

  • Should my spouse be on the LLC? (A single-member LLC pays self-employment tax on all of its profit.)
  • When does an S corp make sense? (Often around $40K a year; the election is due by Dec 31.)
  • Do I owe quarterly estimated taxes, and how much?

Find your floor rate

(Keep + costs) ÷ 0.7 ÷ ~1,000 hrs

Need $50K to live on? Bill about $75K. Over ~1,000 billable hours, that’s about $75 an hour: the floor, not the price.
