Contract checklist

Five clauses every freelance contract needs. Check each one before you sign or send.

Scope

Exactly what’s included, and what isn’t.

Example: “A five-page website: Home, About, Services, Reviews, Contact. Copywriting and photography are not included.”

Revisions

How many rounds, and what extra rounds cost.

Example: “Two rounds of revisions per page. Additional rounds are billed at $__ per hour.”

Payment

50% up front, the rest on delivery, with due dates.

Example: “50% deposit to begin. Remaining 50% due on launch, payable within 14 days.”

Ownership

Rights transfer when the final invoice is paid.

Example: “All rights to the final design transfer to the client once the final invoice is paid in full.”

Kill fee

What you’re owed if they cancel partway through. (In my opinion, not always necessary.)

Example: “If the project is cancelled after work begins, the deposit is non-refundable and completed work is billed at $__.”

When they say “I can get it cheaper”

“He probably can, and that might be the right call. What made you reach out to me?”

Don’t defend your value and don’t drop your price. Listen: their answer is your value, in their words. If the budget really is lower, cut the scope, not the price.

Red flags: pass on the project

  • They can’t explain the problem they want solved.
  • They haggle before you’ve started.
  • They want free samples to “see if you’re a fit,” including in a hiring process.
