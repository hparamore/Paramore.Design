About this property

This home has so much to offer. Just off the main street and within walking distance to just about everything. This home is very spacious inside with a large open kitchen with lots of storage, pantry area, island kitchen butcher countertop, comes with everything you need, range, refrigerator, dishwasher, built in microwave. Sliding doors to the back yard. Just off the kitchen is large laundry room and full bathroom. Enter into a large open living area with a Blaze King wood stove, beautiful laminate flooring. Bedroom off the living area with full bathroom and walk in closet. There are two good sized rooms upstairs that could be used for bedrooms or an office space. Step down off the living area into a very large living area or master bedroom with beautiful french doors open to the back yard. Lots of windows to bring in natural light to the area. There is also a Monitor oil stove that will easily heat the downstairs area. Just when you thought you were done, step into a very large commercial space that was once used as a barber shop with a half bath. Full ceramic tile on the floors and a full wall of windows and a skylight in the ceiling. This is an amazing space! The exterior of the home has metal siding, metal roof and dual pane windows. The front and back yards are both fenced. There is a good sized storage shed in the back yard and a single car carport with a good sized garage as well. Lots of parking on a quiet corner lot. This is a great home wether you are wanting room for family or want to start your own small business this is the place. It is zoned commercial but is grandfathered in as a residence. The best of both worlds. Priced to sell, why pay rent? This is the place for you!
