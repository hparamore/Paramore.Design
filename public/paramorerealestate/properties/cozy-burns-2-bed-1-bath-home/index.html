About this property

Check out this wonderful cozy upper Burns neighborhood home. Great location, very quiet neighborhood. This home has great curb appeal and is in need of some finish work in some places which allows you to add your own personal touch to the home. The home features 1 main bedroom, and the second bedroom is also used as the laundry room. It is in need of some finish work and flooring. The living area is open and spacious with lots of great light from the new dual pane windows that were installed in the home. The kitchen was remodeled and has hardwood cabinets, laminate counter tops and lots of storage. The home is heated with a monitor oil forced air stove. The bathroom was remodeled for handicap access with a large walk in shower and tile flooring. Very cute yard and front deck/patio area. There is a small storage shed in the back as well.
