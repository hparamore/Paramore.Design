About this property

Are you looking for the coziest cottage home ever? This may just be the place. Located literally in the heart of Burns Oregon, walking distance to shopping, downtown, parks and all the activities going on. This beautiful home is ready to move into. Love to garden? Yes, this has a great yard and garden area with some mature trees. Fully fenced and has a great little shop in the back for all your needs. The exterior is in excellent shape with metal siding upgraded dual pane windows, composite roof and a new mini-split heating and cooling system. It also has a wood stove for those extra cold nights. One thing I like most about this home is the huge closed in sunroom area. So many windows, amazing light and sunshine throughout. The kitchen comes with lots of storage space, range, refrigerator as well as the washer and dryer which are also included in the sale. Sleep well knowing that the plumbing was also upgraded with pex plumbing. Nice full bath upstairs. There is also a basement in the home which is accessed from a stairway just off the kitchen. It is a very clean basement, concrete floor, laundry area, tons of storage and a large room which has been used as a bedroom in the past. This home is cozy, clean and ready for you to move right in. Come take a look at this home today, very easy to show.
