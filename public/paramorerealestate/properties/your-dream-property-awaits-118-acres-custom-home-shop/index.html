About this property

Your dream home on a very private 118 acres! NEW INTERIOR PICTURES ATTACHED! This is the one you are looking for. These properties do NOT come along often. This area is very hard to find smaller parcels to build on. You have unobstructed open views of Harney County and the Steens Mountains. Not to mention a custom 1800 Sq, Ft. 3 bed 2 bath home built in 2008 with an oversized garage and a brand new 30'x30' metal shop, power/220, concrete floor, power overhead door. There are two tax lots one is 79.09 acres and the lot with the home on it is 39.09 acres. This would allow you to possibly build on the other lot if desired. The property is fully fenced and cross fenced for cattle or horses. Huge fenced in garden area, chicken coop run. Horse pens and some cattle working area. Water and power to the pasture/cattle area. The custom home features 10 ft ceilings most of the home. So many beautiful large windows with views throughout the home. Large open kitchen with hardwood cabinets, newer stainless steel appliances, range, fridge, microwave and dishwasher all included. Lots of cabinet space. Flooring in the living, dining, kitchen, laundry and office area is all large tile. The bedrooms are very nice laminate wood flooring. Master bedroom has dual sinks and a large walk in shower, also a very large walk in closet. There are two other bedrooms on the other end of the home with a beautiful bathroom in the middle which has a claw foot tub/shower. There are 4 sets of french doors in the home one in the bedroom and three living area. The home has a large covered front and back porch. Heated with an electric heat pump, air conditioning and heat. There is also a wood stove in the front living area, the owner has never used it. Oversized two car fully finished and heated attached garage, insulated pump house inside as well. Roof is composite shingles and siding is hardi-board and wood. Full concrete foundation with large crawl space. The yard is spacious with a fully operational underground sprinkler system. A new well was added in 2022. Septic system was pumped in 2022 and is in good condition. Honestly this home has it all. This is one of the most peaceful properties on the market, incredible privacy, views, wildlife and in very good condition. This property won't last long, call today for a private showing.
