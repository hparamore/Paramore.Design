About this property

Very Rare Fields Oregon 25 Acre Parcel

Welcome to one of the last great outposts. Fields Oregon is an unincorporated community 112 miles south of Burns Oregon. If you are looking for dark skies, peace and quiet and beautiful mountains you may just fall in love with Fields. This is a vacant 25.80 acre lot located just behind the Fields local store which is known for its hamburgers and famous hand made milk shakes. This is a great place to build a home or vacation property. Very close to the Alvord Desert, East side of the Steens Mountain Range and home to many natural hot springs in the area. This property has great access with a gravel road going through the property. The property is also fenced with cattle guards at each end. There is also a season Williams Creek that boarders the property on the south side. There is also power adjacent to the property. The property is zoned RSC-F1. This just may be your future destination property.
