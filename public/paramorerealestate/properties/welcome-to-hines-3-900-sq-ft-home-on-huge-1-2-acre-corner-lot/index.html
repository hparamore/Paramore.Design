About this property

Welcome to Hines! The first time this home has ever hit the market since it was built. Custom 5 bed 3 bath home on 2 quarter acre lots. This property is located on HWY 20 and is zoned commercial. However it is currently being used as a residence. This would make a wonderful home or possible commercial business. The home was built in 1956 and has been well loved for many years by the same family. There are many great features to this home. The exterior features low maintenance metal siding and roof. Large attached two car garage. Covered back patio area, underground sprinkler system most of the property, HUGE yard which you could build a shop, you would have to get approval to get access from the D.O.T. to get access to the additional lot from the highway frontage. Tons of parking as well as RV Parking. This is a very large home at 3,900 Sq, Ft. of living area. There are 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms upstairs and an additional 2 bedrooms and 1 bathroom downstairs. The living area is very large and perfect for entertaining. The kitchen has tons of storage and counter space as well as an attached dining/living area with a fireplace. There is also a wood stove in the living area as well. The bedrooms a large and the master has a walk in closet and its own bathroom. Beautiful wood custom cabinets throughout the home. All the appliances are included in the sale. Refrigerator, microwave, range/oven, dishwasher, wood stove, washer and dryer. Updated electrical throughout the home with updated breaker boxes. The homes main heat source is an electric radiant heat system throughout the upstairs. Huge living area in the basement, large laundry/utility room. Food storage room as well. Oh... Did I mention there is a small workshop room in the basement with its own outside entrance? The home is well insulated. There is a stairway to the attic which is fully insulated as well. Two small work/storage sheds in the back yard. The possibilities are endless with this home. Don't let this one pass you by. Beautiful large home on a very large lot in the heart of Hines.
