About this property

MAJOR PRICE REDUCTION! Ever felt like you needed to get out of town and live in the country? Possible owner carry with a large down payment. This is your chance to make that happen. This is a great little farm ready to go. Just a short drive from Burns Oregon you will find this 162.53 acre farm. Fenced and cross fenced with multiple pasture options for all your animal needs. The home features 3 bed 2 bathrooms on 1, 816 Sq, ft, living area. Huge western style kitchen with range, fridge, dishwasher, large island and dining area with a nice western feel to it. Big vaulted living room with nice light and windows. Built in 1955 and remodeled in 2011 and well insulated, the home is made from cinder blocks and then framed with wood and vinyl siding with a large wrap around porch and composite roof. Sit out and start your day on the huge back deck overlooking the farm. There is also a place to house a friend or two in the detached bunk house with kitchen, heat and living area. Good sized barn in the back with stalls and small workshop/tack room. Power and water to the barn area. Set up to work cattle with a squeeze chute and pens. The property is in two tax lots, one is 80 acres and the other is 82.53 acres. The second tax lot used to have a mobile home located on it but has been removed but is set up with septic, power and well for you to add another home if needed. The property was set up in 20 acre pastures and fenced to be able to rotate cattle, horses or whatever type of animals you like. Lots of mature trees, 2 carport/shop area. Very well insulated pump house, on demand hot water heater in the home as well. This is a very peaceful property, perfect for horses, cattle. Live out your dream today.
