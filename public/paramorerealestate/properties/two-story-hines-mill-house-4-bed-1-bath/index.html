About this property

Don't wait on looking at this large Hines mill house in a wonderful neighborhood. This home has been in the same family for decades. It is close to Hines Park, post office and so much more. This home features 4 bedrooms and 1 full bath. Open living area with lots of windows, great light throughout the home. Separate dining area and fully functional kitchen with lots of storage. Two bedrooms downstairs and two bedrooms upstairs. There is a nice enclosed front porch and a separate laundry area off the enclosed back porch. Very large storage room upstairs with built in shelving and an additional storage area in the partial basement. The home has multiple heat sources, oil furnace, mini-splits with 3 heads, one in the living area, dining/kitchen and one in the master bedroom. There are also electric baseboard heaters located in the home. Updated electrical panel/breaker box, wood siding, single pane windows, composite roof. The home is in good shape but is a bit dated, just waiting for your own personal touch. All appliances are included in the sale. This home is just waiting for the next Chip and JoAnna Gaines to come in and make it your own. The price is right so don't wait on this one. Why pay rent when you can own this home.
