About this property

This home is in one of the better locations in the City of Hines. Just a block away from the beautiful Hines Park and the golf course. If you are a first time buyer or someone who wants to settle in for the long haul. This home is very efficient, close to everything and has a large yard. The home features a metal roof, metal siding and partial stone front area. Dual pane windows. Fenced back yard, covered carport and garden area with fruit trees. The interior features three different types of heat, newer mini split system which provides heating and air conditioning, also an oil forced air furnace in the basement with a stove to allow you to burn wood through the system as well. The main bedroom is on the main level, and an additional bedroom upstairs with a large storage room which has been used as a bedroom in the past. There is also a possible third bedroom on the lower level which is currently being used as a guitar room. One full bathroom on the main level. The living room is very open and has a huge picture window. The kitchen is open and includes all the appliances, range, fridge, washer/dryer. Just off the kitchen is a nice sized dining area. Laundry room is off of the back porch. There is an exterior walk in basement that is unfinished but has lots of storage. What I love about this home is how efficient it is to heat and cool as well as the location. I can easily walk to the park or the golf course. Excellent neighborhood. Should qualify for most type of financing. WHY PAY RENT! READY TO MOVE INTO!
