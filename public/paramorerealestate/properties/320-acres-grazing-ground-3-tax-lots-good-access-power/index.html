About this property

LOOKING FOR GRAZING LAND? 320 Acres on Sandhill Lane in Burns Oregon. This property is pretty secluded but does have good access off of Newton Road and Sandhill Lane. The property is being used for grazing cattle and is leased at the moment. At closing the lease will be terminated. There are two very old and nearly destroyed single wide mobile homes on the property. These are "NOT" in livable condition. There is a domestic well on the property which at one time was used for the home but now is being used for watering cattle. There is a power pole on the property as well. The perimeter of the property is fenced, however it is fenced around the two mobile homes with electric fencing which belongs to the person leasing the property. The property is being sold in "AS IS" condition. There are three tax lots, Zoned EFRU-2. Tax Lot 2800 is 80 Acres, 2900 is 80 Acres, and 3000 is 160 Acres. You could build a home on each of these parcels if you wanted to. The property is in farm deferral presently. Looking for privacy? This is a very private area on a very seldom used dirt road. Directions to the property are as follows: Take Highway 78 East, turn left on to Newton Road, go 4 miles then turn left onto Sandhill Lane, drive 1 mile the property is on the right side.
