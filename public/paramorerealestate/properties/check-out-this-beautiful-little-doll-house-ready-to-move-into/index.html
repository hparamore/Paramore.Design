About this property

This is a beautiful little doll house READY TO MOVE INTO! Come look this cute home over. Great curb appeal, newer siding, dual pane windows/storm windows, newer composite roof, new exterior paint. Home has a good concrete foundation. Nestled on a quiet corner lot in Burns Oregon. You will love the fenced in yard and garden shed, as well as the sweet workshop/garage with concrete floor, power and a nice deck off the back. The interior features 3, possible 4 bedrooms or an office. Nice open living room area with laminate flooring. Wonderful light throughout the home with many windows. A good sized galley kitchen with range, fridge, dining area. Full bath with claw foot tub/shower, nice window, vanity good lighting. Don't forget about the warmth of the sunroom entrance with tons of windows and sunlight. There is a laundry room off the back of the kitchen with all hookups. Bottom line, this home is full of love and ready for its next adventure. The home should qualify for most types of financing and is not in the flood zone.
