About this property

A very private 2 bed, 1 bath home on a dead end road in Burns Oregon. This home is very secluded and has numerous trees. This has been a great rental home for the past 12 years. Now it can be yours. The home features metal siding, composite roof. It is built on a solid concrete slab. The plumbing and electrical have all been updated.
