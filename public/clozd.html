A sincere aside

I treat users like fans.

Fans First. Entertain Always.

Jesse Cole · Founder, Savannah Bananas & Banana Ball

That's the entire Banana Ball operating principle: put the fan experience ahead of every spreadsheet, and trust the spreadsheet to catch up. For ten years I've built product on the same conviction.

Every detail I work on — empty states, microcopy, the way an error reads, the haptic at the moment a deal closes — exists to make the user feel respected. Not to satisfy the org chart. Not to hit a quarterly target. To make the person on the other end of the screen feel like the team building this actually cares.

Users reward that. They come back, they refer friends, they ride out the rough patches with you. It isn't idealism — it's the most defensible long-term strategy a product team can adopt. And it's the one I'd bring to Clozd.
