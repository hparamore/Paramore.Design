Role Solo Designer-Developer
Timeline 3 Weeks
Stack React Native, Firebase, Claude API
Year 2026
HERO IMAGE

One tool, four subscriptions retired.

Brandon's crew was running on paper checklists, fuel receipts in a Tupperware, equipment lists in someone's head, and four separate SaaS subscriptions duct-taped together. I rode along for two days, designed for the truck instead of the office, and shipped a single tool in three weeks that replaced the whole stack.

EQUIPMENT · DESKTOP
Equipment · Inventory What used to live on a clipboard now lives in a search bar. Photos, condition, location, and last-serviced — one tap away.

The yard, on the phone.

Walk the yard, find the saw, log the problem. The crew uses this in gloves between jobs — every interaction is thumb-reachable and survives spotty rural service.
