A sincere aside

I live golden, too.

Fans First. Entertain Always.

Jesse Cole · Founder, Savannah Bananas & Banana Ball

That's the entire Banana Ball operating principle: put the fan experience ahead of every spreadsheet, and trust the spreadsheet to catch up. It's almost the same idea as Veras's "live golden" — treat every person, teammate, customer, resident, like the most important person in the room.

For ten years I've built product on that exact conviction. Every detail I work on — empty states, microcopy, the way an error reads, the haptic at the moment a CNA opens her schedule on her phone — exists to make the user feel respected. Not to satisfy the org chart. Not to hit a quarterly target. To make the person on the other end of the screen feel like the team building this actually cares.

Users reward that. They come back, they refer their teammates, they ride out the rough patches with you. It isn't idealism — it's the most defensible long-term strategy a product team can adopt. It's also the one Veras has explicitly built its culture around. Which is why I'm writing this page in the first place.
